Key findings

  1. Discounts are the main cause of lost profit. Margin falls from 22.2% on undiscounted orders to 7.6% when the discount is above 10%. All 23 loss-making orders had discounts above 10%, including one order at 35% that lost ₹4,578.
  2. ₹71.8 L (31% of sales) is still uncollected: ₹48.3 L is pending and ₹23.5 L is overdue across 126 orders. Neha (29) and Sana (27) have the most overdue orders.
  3. Hardware drives volume (36.6% of sales), while Plumbing (19.8%) and Electrical (18.9%) earn the best margins. Safety has a margin of only 9.5%. Product 4 is the 5th-largest product by sales but earns just 5.6%.
  4. Sales are steady but not growing. Monthly sales range from ₹15.3 L to ₹22.1 L. May was the peak and July the lowest month (−25% vs June), and dips repeat in Feb–Mar and September.
  5. The customer base is healthy and diversified. The top 20% of customers bring in only 29% of sales, and Retailers make up 51%. Varanasi is the largest city (₹41.2 L). Amit is the top salesperson (₹52.5 L, 16.7% margin).

Recommendations

  1. Cap discounts at 10%. Anything higher should need manager approval. This alone would have prevented every loss-making order in 2025.
  2. Start a collections drive on the ₹23.5 L overdue amount, beginning with the top 10 overdue customers (about ₹8.9 L). Link part of salesperson incentives to collections.
  3. Reprice or renegotiate supplier costs for Product 4, 1, 2, 18 and the Safety range, and push higher-margin Plumbing and Electrical products.
  4. Run targeted promotions in slow months (Feb–Mar, Jul, Sep) to smooth out sales.
